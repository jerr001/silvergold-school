Admissions

Admission Process

  1. Submit an application form (available at the school office or online)
  2. Provide necessary documents (birth certificate, previous school records, etc.)
  3. Attend an entrance assessment/interview
  4. Receive admission decision
  5. Complete registration and payment

Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate
  • Medical report
  • Previous school transcripts (for transfers)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Parent/Guardian identification

Note: Contact the school for detailed fees information.
