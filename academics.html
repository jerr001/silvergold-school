Academics

Crèche

Our Crèche program provides a nurturing environment for children aged 1-3 years. We focus on early cognitive development, motor skills, and social interaction.

Subjects: Basic motor skills, language development, sensory activities

Teaching Approach: Play-based learning with structured activities

Nursery

For children aged 3-5 years, our Nursery program builds on early learning foundations while introducing basic academic concepts.

Subjects: English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Arts, Physical Education

Teaching Approach: Interactive and fun learning methods

Primary

Primary education (ages 5-11) covers comprehensive learning across all major subjects with a focus on building strong foundations.

Subjects: English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Civic Education, Computer Studies, French, Music, Art, Physical Education

Teaching Approach: Student-centered learning with practical applications

Junior Secondary School (JSS)

JSS (ages 11-14) prepares students for senior secondary with a broad curriculum and introduction to specialized subjects.

Subjects: English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, Social Studies, Civic Education, Computer Studies, French, Business Studies, Home Economics, Physical Education

Teaching Approach: Inquiry-based learning and critical thinking development

Senior Secondary School (SSS)

SSS (ages 14-18) offers specialized streams in Science, Arts, and Commercial studies, preparing students for higher education.

Subjects: English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Literature, Government, Economics, Accounting, Geography, Further Mathematics, Computer Science

Teaching Approach: Advanced conceptual learning with practical applications and exam preparation
