About Silvergold British High School

School History

Silvergold British High School was established in 2005 with the vision of providing world-class education in Nigeria. Over the years, we have grown from a small institution to a leading school offering comprehensive education from Crèche to Senior Secondary School.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a leading educational institution that nurtures excellence, integrity, and leadership in every student.

Mission: To provide holistic education that combines academic rigor with moral development, preparing students for success in life.

Core Values

  • Excellence in all endeavors
  • Integrity and honesty
  • Respect for diversity
  • Commitment to community
  • Innovation in teaching
