About Silvergold British High School

School History

Silvergold British High School was established in 2005 with the vision of providing world-class education in Nigeria. Over the years, we have grown from a small institution to a leading school offering comprehensive education from Crèche to Senior Secondary School.

Mission

To provide a high quality, Christ centered education that nurtures academic excellence, integrity, and global competence, empowering students to become responsible leaders and lifelong learners.

Vision

To be a world class institution recognized for excellence in education, character development, and international student success.

Our Core Values – “G.R.E.A.T.”

At SilverGold British High School, our values shape not just what we teach, but who our students become.

  • G – Godliness
    A commitment to Christ centered living, reflecting strong moral character, spiritual growth, and values rooted in love, humility, and righteousness.
  • R – Responsibility
    The ability to be dependable and accountable for one’s actions, decisions, and duties, both in academics and in everyday life.
  • E – Excellence
    A dedication to achieving the highest standards in academics, behavior, and personal development, with a mindset of continuous improvement.
  • A – Accountability
    The willingness to take ownership of one’s actions, accept consequences, and demonstrate honesty and integrity at all times.
  • T – Tenacity
    The determination to persevere through challenges, remain focused on goals, and never give up in the pursuit of success.
